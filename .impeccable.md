## Design Context

### Users
Research collaborators reviewing deformation-detection experiments, methods, quantitative results, and interactive evidence.

### Brand Personality
Readable, precise, restrained. Readers should be able to inspect evidence and assess the results confidently.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the current published PDI review site: light neutral backgrounds, dark text, blue links, clear comparison pictures and tables. Prioritize highly readable research documentation.

### Design Principles
- Explain methods visually before presenting the complete case gallery.
- Use synchronized, equally framed before/after comparisons.
- Keep scores, cohorts, label policies, and source evidence explicit.
- Preserve interactive replay inspection and make controls readable on small screens.
- Stage pair-selection results, Simple3D examples, and architecture in that order.
